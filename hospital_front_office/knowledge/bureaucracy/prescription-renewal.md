# Prescription Renewals

## Procedure for Prescription Renewals

### Step-by-Step
1. Go to the **Prescription Office** — Ground Floor, Wing D, Window 2
2. Bring your current prescription, ID, and tessera sanitaria
3. The pharmacist will verify the prescription and issue a renewal
4. Pay any applicable co-pay (ticket)

### Office Hours
- Monday to Friday: 08:00 - 16:00
- Saturday: 08:00 - 12:00

### Requirements
- Original prescription must not be older than 30 days
- Some medications require a new doctor's visit for renewal
- Controlled substances cannot be renewed — new prescription required

### Co-Pay (Ticket)
- Standard co-pay: €2.50 per package
- Exemptions available (see exemptions.md)
- Maximum monthly co-pay: €50

### Notes
- Prescriptions can be renewed up to 3 times before a new doctor's visit is required
- Chronic medication patients can request 6-month renewals at their specialist visit
