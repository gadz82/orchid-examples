# Medical Records Request

## How to Request Medical Records

### Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Go to the **Medical Records Office** — Ground Floor, Wing D, Window 3
2. Bring:
   - Valid ID
   - Tessera sanitaria
   - Completed request form (available at the office or downloadable from the hospital website)
3. Submit the request form
4. Processing time: 7-15 business days
5. Pick up in person or request delivery by mail (additional fee applies)

### Office Hours
- Monday to Friday: 09:00 - 12:00

### What You Can Request
- Visit reports and discharge summaries
- Laboratory test results
- Imaging reports (X-ray, MRI, CT)
- Surgical reports
- Full medical history summary

### Fees
- Standard request: €10
- Urgent request (3 business days): €25
- Mail delivery: €5 additional
- Digital copy (USB): €8

### Urgent Requests
- Ask for the "urgent request" procedure at Window 3
- Requires justification (e.g., transfer to another hospital, legal proceedings)

### Legal Rights
- Patients have the right to access their complete medical records
- Records are retained for a minimum of 10 years after the last visit
