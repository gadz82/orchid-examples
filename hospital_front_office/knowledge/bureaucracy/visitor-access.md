# Visitor Access and Badges

## Visitor Badge and Access Procedures

### Standard Visiting Hours
- Monday to Friday: 10:00 - 12:00, 14:00 - 18:00
- Saturday: 10:00 - 12:00
- Sunday: 14:00 - 18:00

### Visitor Badge
1. Go to the **Information Desk** — Main Entrance
2. Provide:
   - Your name
   - Name of the patient you are visiting
   - Department and room number (if known)
3. Receive a visitor badge (must be worn at all times)
4. Return the badge when leaving

### Restricted Access Areas
- **Oncology (4th Floor, Wing B):** Visitor badge + patient authorization required
- **ICU:** Limited to 2 visitors per patient, 30-minute slots
- **Pediatrics:** Only parents/guardians allowed (no general visitors)

### Special Permissions
- Extended visiting hours for critical patients: request at the department nurse station
- Overnight stay permission: requires doctor's authorization

### Visitor Guidelines
- Maximum 2 visitors per patient at a time
- Children under 12 not permitted in most departments
- No food or drinks in patient rooms (except Pediatrics)
- Mobile phones must be on silent

### Visitor Parking
- Available near Entrance B
- €2/hour, first 30 minutes free
- Disabled parking spots reserved
