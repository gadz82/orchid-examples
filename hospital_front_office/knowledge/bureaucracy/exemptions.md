# Exemption Procedures (Esenzioni)

## What Are Exemptions?
Exemptions (esenzioni) waive the co-pay (ticket) for certain medical services based on income, age, or medical condition.

## Exemption Categories

### Income-Based (E01-E04)
- **E01:** Household income below €8,263.31
- **E02:** Household income below €11,362.05 with dependent family members
- **E03:** Household income below €8,263.31 with dependent family members
- **E04:** Unemployed, household income below €8,263.31

### Age-Based
- **Children under 6:** Automatic exemption
- **Adults over 65:** Automatic exemption (with income verification)

### Condition-Based (Chronic Diseases)
- Specific exemption codes for chronic conditions (e.g., diabetes, hypertension, cancer)
- Code assigned by your GP or specialist
- Valid for 2 years, renewable

### Pregnancy
- All pregnancy-related services are exempt
- Valid from conception until 6 months postpartum

## How to Apply
1. Go to the **Exemption Office** — Ground Floor, Wing D, Window 5
2. Bring:
   - Valid ID and tessera sanitaria
   - Income documentation (ISEE certificate) for income-based exemptions
   - Medical certificate from GP for condition-based exemptions
3. Receive your exemption code
4. Present the code at every visit or prescription renewal
