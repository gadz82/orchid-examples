# Required Documents for Hospital Visits

## Standard Visit Documentation

### Always Required
- **Valid ID** — Passport or national identity card
- **Tessera sanitaria** — Italian health card (mandatory for all visits)
- **Referral letter** — From your GP or specialist (if applicable)

### Department-Specific Requirements

| Department | Additional Documents |
|------------|---------------------|
| Cardiology | Previous ECG results (if available) |
| Neurology | Previous EEG or MRI reports |
| Orthopedics | X-rays or imaging from referring doctor |
| Oncology | Biopsy reports, previous treatment records |
| General Surgery | Pre-op blood work (if scheduled for surgery) |

### First Visit vs. Follow-Up
- **First visit:** All documents above + completed new-patient form
- **Follow-up:** ID, tessera sanitaria, and any new test results

### Where to Submit Documents
- Registration Office — Ground Floor, Wing D, Window 1
- Or bring directly to the department on the day of your visit
