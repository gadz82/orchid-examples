# Appointments — Change or Cancel

## How to Change or Cancel Appointments

### Changing an Appointment
1. Call the **Appointment Center**: 06-1234-5620
2. Or use the online portal: prenotazioni.ospedale.it
3. Provide your appointment reference number
4. Select a new available date and time
5. Receive confirmation via SMS or email

### Canceling an Appointment
1. Call the Appointment Center: 06-1234-5620
2. Or use the online portal
3. Or visit the Registration Office in person
4. Cancel at least 48 hours before the appointment to avoid a no-show record

### Office Hours (Appointment Center)
- Monday to Friday: 08:00 - 18:00
- Saturday: 08:00 - 12:00

### No-Show Policy
- 3 no-shows within 12 months: temporary suspension of booking privileges (30 days)
- Emergency appointments are not affected

### Same-Day Changes
- Call before 08:00 on the day of the appointment
- Subject to availability
- Not all departments support same-day changes

### Online Portal
- Register at prenotazioni.ospedale.it with your tessera sanitaria number
- View, change, or cancel appointments
- Receive SMS and email reminders
