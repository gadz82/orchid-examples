# Medical Certificates

## How to Obtain Medical Certificates

### Types of Certificates Available
- **Sick leave certificate** — For employment absence
- **Fitness to work certificate** — For return to work after illness
- **Sports fitness certificate** — For athletic activities
- **Travel health certificate** — For international travel
- **Disability assessment certificate** — For benefits applications

### Procedure
1. Book an appointment with the **Certification Office** — Ground Floor, Wing D, Window 4
2. Bring your ID, tessera sanitaria, and any relevant medical records
3. The doctor will examine you and issue the certificate
4. Pay the certificate fee (see below)

### Office Hours
- Monday to Friday: 09:00 - 12:00
- Appointments required — call 06-1234-5699

### Fees
- Sick leave certificate: €15
- Sports fitness certificate: €30
- Travel health certificate: €25
- Disability assessment: €50 (includes specialist evaluation)

### Processing Time
- Standard certificates: issued immediately after examination
- Disability assessment: 7-15 business days
