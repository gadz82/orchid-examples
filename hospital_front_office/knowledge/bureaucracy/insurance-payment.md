# Insurance and Payment Procedures

## Insurance Coverage

### Accepted Insurance Plans
- Italian National Health Service (SSN) — covers most services
- Private insurance plans — check with the billing office
- International insurance — pre-authorization may be required

### Payment Methods
- Cash
- Credit/debit card (Visa, Mastercard)
- Bank transfer (for large amounts)

## Co-Pay (Ticket) System

### Standard Co-Pay Rates
- Specialist visit: €36.15
- Laboratory test: €2.50 - €10.00 per test
- Imaging (X-ray, ultrasound): €36.15
- MRI/CT scan: €80.00 - €120.00

### Exemptions
- Children under 6 and adults over 65
- Low-income households (ISEE below threshold)
- Chronic conditions (specific exemption codes)
- Pregnancy-related services
- See exemptions.md for details

## Payment Plans
- Available for amounts over €200
- Up to 12 monthly installments
- Apply at the billing office with proof of income
