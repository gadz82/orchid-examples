# Patient Registration

## How to Register as a New Patient

### Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Go to the **Registration Office** — Ground Floor, Wing D, Window 1
2. Bring the required documents (see below)
3. Fill out the registration form (available at the office or downloadable from the hospital website)
4. Submit the form and documents to the clerk
5. Receive your patient ID card (issued immediately)

### Office Hours
- Monday to Friday: 08:00 - 14:00
- Saturday: 08:00 - 12:00
- Sunday: Closed

### Required Documents
- Valid ID (passport or national identity card)
- Tessera sanitaria (Italian health card)
- Proof of residence (utility bill or residence certificate)
- Referral letter from your GP (if applicable)

### Notes
- Registration is free of charge
- Patient ID card is valid for 5 years
- Lost cards can be replaced at the same office with a €5 fee
