# Complaints and Feedback

## How to File a Complaint or Provide Feedback

### Complaint Procedure
1. Obtain a complaint form from:
   - Information desk (main entrance)
   - Any department reception
   - Hospital website (downloadable PDF)
2. Fill out the form with:
   - Your name and contact information (optional — anonymous complaints accepted)
   - Date and location of the incident
   - Description of the issue
   - Names of staff involved (if known)
3. Submit the form to:
   - **Patient Relations Office** — Ground Floor, Wing D, Window 6
   - Or drop in the suggestion box at any floor lobby
   - Or email: reclami@ospedale.it

### Response Timeline
- Acknowledgment: within 5 business days
- Investigation: 15-30 business days
- Written response: within 30 business days

### Feedback (Positive)
- Submit via the same channels as complaints
- Positive feedback is shared with the relevant department
- Staff recognition program based on patient feedback
