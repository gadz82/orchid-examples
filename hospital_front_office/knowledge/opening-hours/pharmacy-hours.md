# Pharmacy Hours

## Hospital Pharmacy

### Opening Hours
- **Monday to Friday:** 08:00 - 18:00
- **Saturday:** 08:00 - 13:00
- **Sunday:** Closed

### Location
- Pharmacy — Ground Floor, Wing A, near the main entrance

### Services
- Dispensing of hospital-prescribed medications
- Over-the-counter medications
- Medical supplies (bandages, thermometers, etc.)
- Medication counseling

### After-Hours
- For urgent medication needs after hours, use the pharmacy on duty
- Check farmacie-di-turno.it for the nearest open pharmacy
- Emergency medications available at the ED

### Insurance
- Most medications covered by SSN with applicable co-pay
- Bring your tessera sanitaria and prescription
- Exemption codes accepted (see exemptions.md)
