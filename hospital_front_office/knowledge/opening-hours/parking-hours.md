# Parking Hours

## Parking Facilities

### Main Parking Garage
- **Location:** Underground, accessible from Entrance B
- **Hours:** 24/7
- **Rates:**
  - First 30 minutes: Free
  - €2/hour
  - Daily maximum: €15
  - Monthly pass: €80 (available at the information desk)

### Disabled Parking
- Reserved spots near Entrance B (ground level)
- Valid disabled parking permit required
- Same rates apply

### Visitor Parking
- Surface lot near Entrance C
- **Hours:** 06:00 - 22:00
- **Rates:** €1.50/hour

### Staff Parking
- Separate lot behind the building (Entrance D)
- Staff badge required for access

### Electric Vehicle Charging
- 4 charging stations in the underground garage
- €0.30/kWh
- App-based payment
