# Cafeteria Hours

## Hospital Cafeteria

### Opening Hours
- **Monday to Friday:** 07:00 - 20:00
- **Saturday:** 07:00 - 18:00
- **Sunday:** 08:00 - 16:00

### Location
- Cafeteria — Ground Floor, Wing A, near the main entrance

### Meal Service Times
- **Breakfast:** 07:00 - 10:00
- **Lunch:** 12:00 - 14:30
- **Snack:** 15:30 - 17:00
- **Dinner:** 18:00 - 19:30

### Menu
- Hot meals, sandwiches, salads, desserts
- Vegetarian and gluten-free options available
- Dietary restrictions accommodated — ask staff

### Vending Machines
- Available 24/7 on every floor
- Snacks, drinks, and basic necessities
- Located near elevator banks on each floor

### Payment
- Cash and card accepted
- Meal vouchers accepted
