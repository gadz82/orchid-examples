# Emergency Department Hours

## Emergency Department (ED)
- **Open 24/7** — Always accessible
- **Entrance:** Left side of the building, marked with red cross signs

## Triage Hours
- Triage nurse on duty 24/7
- Wait times vary by triage code (see triage-codes.md)

## Non-Urgent Cases
- For non-urgent issues, consider:
  - **Guardia Medica** (out-of-hours GP): 20:00 - 08:00 weekdays, 24/7 weekends
  - **Pharmacy on duty:** Rotating schedule, check farmacie-di-turno.it
  - **Your GP:** During regular office hours

## ED Contact
- Phone: 06-1234-5699 (emergency line)
- Do not call for non-urgent inquiries
