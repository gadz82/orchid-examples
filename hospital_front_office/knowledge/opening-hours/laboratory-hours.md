# Laboratory Hours

## Blood Draw and Laboratory Services

### Blood Draw Hours
- **Monday to Friday:** 07:30 - 10:30 (fasting required)
- **Saturday:** 07:30 - 10:00 (fasting required)
- **Sunday:** Closed

### Results Collection
- **Monday to Friday:** 11:00 - 16:00
- **Saturday:** 11:00 - 13:00
- Online results available at referti.ospedale.it (48-72 hours after draw)

### Location
- Laboratory — Basement, Wing B (take Elevator B1 to B-1)

### Special Tests
- **Glucose tolerance test:** By appointment, call 06-1234-5625
- **Allergy testing:** Tuesday and Thursday, 08:00 - 12:00
- **Hormone panels:** Monday, Wednesday, Friday, 07:30 - 09:00

### Fasting Requirements
- 8-12 hours fasting for standard blood work
- Water is allowed
- Take regular medications unless instructed otherwise by your doctor
