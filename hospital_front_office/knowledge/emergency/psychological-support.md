# Psychological Emergency Support

## Mental Health Emergency Guidance

### When to Seek Emergency Mental Health Support
- Suicidal thoughts or plans
- Severe panic attacks that do not subside
- Psychotic episodes (hallucinations, delusions)
- Severe self-harm urges
- Acute stress reaction after trauma

### Emergency Contacts
- **118** — For immediate life-threatening situations
- **Telefono Amico:** 02-2327-2327 (24/7 listening service)
- **Samaritans Italy:** 06-7720-8977 (13:00 - 22:00)
- **Emergency Psychiatric Service:** 06-1234-5650 (hospital line)

### What to Do
1. **If you are in crisis:**
   - Call 118 or go to the Emergency Department
   - Do not stay alone — call a trusted person
   - Remove access to means of self-harm

2. **If someone else is in crisis:**
   - Stay with them, do not leave them alone
   - Listen without judgment
   - Call 118 if they are in immediate danger
   - Do not try to handle a severe crisis alone

### Hospital Psychiatric Services
- **Location:** 3rd Floor, Wing B (adjacent to Neurology)
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday, 09:00 - 17:00
- **Emergency psychiatric evaluation:** Available 24/7 via the ED
- **No appointment needed for emergencies**

### What to Expect at the ED
- Triage assessment (mental health emergencies are taken seriously)
- Psychiatric evaluation by an on-call psychiatrist
- Possible referral to inpatient or outpatient services
- Confidential — your information is protected

### After the Emergency
- Follow-up appointments will be scheduled
- Community mental health center (CSM) referral provided
- Crisis hotline numbers given for ongoing support
