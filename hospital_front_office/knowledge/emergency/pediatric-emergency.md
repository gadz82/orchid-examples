# Pediatric Emergency

## Pediatric Emergency Department

### Location
- Pediatrics Emergency — Ground Floor, Wing A (separate entrance marked with colorful signs)

### Hours
- Open 24/7 for children under 14

### Triage for Children
- **Code RED:** Immediate — breathing difficulty, seizures, severe trauma, unconsciousness
- **Code ORANGE:** < 15 min — high fever (> 39.5°C) with lethargy, severe dehydration, suspected fracture
- **Code YELLOW:** < 60 min — moderate fever, vomiting, minor fractures, cuts needing stitches
- **Code GREEN:** < 120 min — mild fever, cold symptoms, minor rashes
- **Code WHITE:** Redirected — prescription renewals, certificate requests

### What to Bring
- Child's ID or health card
- Parent/guardian ID
- Any medications the child is taking
- Vaccination record (if available)

### Parent Presence
- One parent/guardian must accompany the child at all times
- Parent can stay during examination and treatment
- Siblings under 12 not permitted in the emergency area

### When to Call 118 for a Child
- Breathing difficulty or cessation
- Seizures lasting more than 5 minutes
- Unconsciousness or unresponsiveness
- Severe allergic reaction
- Severe trauma (fall from height, car accident)
- Suspected poisoning
