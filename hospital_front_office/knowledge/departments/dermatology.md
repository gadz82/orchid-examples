# Dermatology Department

## Location
- **Floor:** 2nd Floor
- **Wing:** Wing B
- **Nearest Elevator:** Elevator Bank B1
- **Nearest Stairs:** Stair B (central corridor)

## Directions from Main Entrance
1. Enter through main doors
2. Go straight past the information desk
3. Take Elevator B1 or Stair B to 2nd floor
4. Turn right, follow skin-health signs
5. Dermatology is next to the dermatology lab, rooms 230-236

## Accessibility
- Wheelchair accessible via Elevator B1
- Dermatology lab on same floor
