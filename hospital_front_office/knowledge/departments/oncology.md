# Oncology Department

## Location
- **Floor:** 4th Floor
- **Wing:** Wing B
- **Nearest Elevator:** Elevator Bank B1
- **Nearest Stairs:** Stair B (central corridor)

## Directions from Main Entrance
1. Enter through main doors
2. Go straight past the information desk
3. Take Elevator B1 or Stair B to 4th floor
4. Turn left, follow oncology signs
5. Oncology is in the restricted access area, rooms 420-430

## Accessibility
- Wheelchair accessible via Elevator B1
- Chemotherapy unit on same floor (08:00-16:00)
- Restricted access — visitor badge required
