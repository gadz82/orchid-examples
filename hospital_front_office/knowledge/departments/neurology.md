# Neurology Department

## Location
- **Floor:** 3rd Floor
- **Wing:** Wing B
- **Nearest Elevator:** Elevator Bank B1
- **Nearest Stairs:** Stair B (central corridor)

## Directions from Main Entrance
1. Enter through main doors
2. Go straight past the information desk
3. Take Elevator B1 or Stair B to 3rd floor
4. Turn right, follow blue brain signs
5. Neurology is adjacent to Radiology, rooms 310-318

## Accessibility
- Wheelchair accessible via Elevator B1
- EEG room on same floor, no stairs required
