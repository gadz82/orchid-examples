# Radiology Department

## Location
- **Floor:** Basement (B-1)
- **Wing:** Wing B
- **Nearest Elevator:** Elevator Bank B1 (go to B-1)
- **Nearest Stairs:** Stair B (central corridor, down to basement)

## Directions from Main Entrance
1. Enter through main doors
2. Go straight past the information desk
3. Take Elevator B1 down to B-1 (basement)
4. Turn left, follow radiology signs
5. Radiology is on the left, rooms B101-B110

## Accessibility
- Wheelchair accessible via Elevator B1
- X-ray, MRI, and CT scan rooms on same floor
