# Cardiology Department

## Location
- **Floor:** 2nd Floor
- **Wing:** Wing A
- **Nearest Elevator:** Elevator Bank A2
- **Nearest Stairs:** Stair A (main atrium)

## Directions from Main Entrance
1. Enter through main doors
2. Turn right at the information desk
3. Take Stair A or Elevator A2 to 2nd floor
4. Turn left, follow red heart signs
5. Cardiology is at the end of corridor A2, rooms 214-220

## Accessibility
- Wheelchair accessible via Elevator A2
- Reserved parking spots near entrance B
