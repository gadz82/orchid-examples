# Ophthalmology Department

- **Floor:** 2nd Floor
- **Wing:** Wing C
- **Nearest Elevator:** Elevator Bank C1
- **Nearest Stairs:** Stair C (west atrium, near stairwell C)

1. Enter through main doors
2. Turn left at the information desk
3. Take Elevator C1 or Stair C to 2nd floor
4. Turn left, follow eye-vision signs
5. Ophthalmology is near stairwell C, rooms 240-246
