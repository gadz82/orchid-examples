# Gastroenterology Department

## Location
- **Floor:** 3rd Floor
- **Wing:** Wing C
- **Nearest Elevator:** Elevator Bank C1
- **Nearest Stairs:** Stair C (west atrium)

## Directions from Main Entrance
1. Enter through main doors
2. Turn left at the information desk
3. Take Elevator C1 or Stair C to 3rd floor
4. Turn right, follow digestive-health signs
5. Gastroenterology is at the end of corridor C3, rooms 340-348

## Accessibility
- Wheelchair accessible via Elevator C1
- Endoscopy unit on same floor
