# ENT Department (Otorinolaringoiatria)

## Location
- **Floor:** 3rd Floor
- **Wing:** Wing A
- **Nearest Elevator:** Elevator Bank A2
- **Nearest Stairs:** Stair A (main atrium)

## Directions from Main Entrance
1. Enter through main doors
2. Turn right at the information desk
3. Take Elevator A2 or Stair A to 3rd floor
4. Follow blue signs to Wing A
5. ENT is at the start of corridor A3, rooms 301-306

## Accessibility
- Wheelchair accessible via Elevator A2
- Audiology testing room on same floor
