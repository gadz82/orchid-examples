# Urology Department

## Location
- **Floor:** 4th Floor
- **Wing:** Wing A (East Wing)
- **Nearest Elevator:** Elevator Bank A2
- **Nearest Stairs:** Stair A (main atrium)

## Directions from Main Entrance
1. Enter through main doors
2. Turn right at the information desk
3. Take Elevator A2 or Stair A to 4th floor
4. Turn right, follow urology signs
5. Urology is on the east wing, rooms 410-416

## Accessibility
- Wheelchair accessible via Elevator A2
- Uroflowmetry testing on same floor (Tue/Thu)
