# Slide Submission

## How to Submit Your Slides

### Online Submission
- **Portal:** speaker.techconf2026.com/slides
- **Deadline:** 48 hours before your session
- **Login:** Use the credentials from your speaker confirmation email

### Accepted Formats
- **PDF** (preferred) — 16:9 aspect ratio
- **PPTX** — 16:9 aspect ratio
- **Keynote** — export to PDF before uploading

### File Requirements
- Maximum file size: 100 MB
- Embedded fonts must be included
- Videos: Provide separately in MP4 (H.264) format
- Links: Test all hyperlinks before submission

### On-Site Submission
- USB drive accepted at the Green Room
- Bring a backup copy
- AV technician will load and test your slides

### After Submission
- You will receive a confirmation email
- You can update your slides up to 24 hours before your session
- Changes within 24 hours require AV technician assistance

### Late Submissions
- Slides submitted after the deadline may not be pre-loaded
- Bring a USB backup to the green room
- AV technician will do their best to accommodate

## Presentation Tips
- Test your slides on a 16:9 display before the conference
- Use high-contrast colors for readability
- Keep text minimal — let your talk carry the detail
- Include your contact info on the last slide
