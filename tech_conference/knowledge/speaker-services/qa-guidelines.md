# Post-Session Q&A Guidelines

## Q&A Best Practices

### Format
- **Track rooms (A-E):** 5-10 minutes Q&A after each session
- **Main Hall:** 10-15 minutes Q&A after keynotes and panels
- **Workshops:** Q&A integrated throughout the session

### Microphone Protocol
- Speaker uses lapel mic for answers
- Audience Q&A uses handheld mic (passed by session moderator)
- Repeat the question into your mic before answering

### Handling Questions
- If you don't know the answer, say so — offer to follow up
- Deflect overly long "questions" that are actually statements
- Encourage diverse questioners
- It's OK to say "let's continue this conversation after the session"

### Time Management
- The session moderator will signal when Q&A time is ending
- Aim for 2-3 questions minimum
- If no questions, have a backup question ready

### After the Session
- Stay for 5-10 minutes for individual questions
- Share your contact info (Twitter, email, website)
- Direct attendees to the slide submission portal for slide access

### Recording
- All sessions are recorded including Q&A
- You can opt out of recording at check-in
- Recorded Q&A will be published on the conference website
