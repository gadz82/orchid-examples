# Rehearsal Booking

## How to Book a Rehearsal Slot

### Rehearsal Slots
- **Duration:** 15 minutes per speaker
- **Timing:** Scheduled before your session (typically 1-2 hours before)
- **Location:** Your assigned room

### What to Rehearse
- Slide transitions and timing
- Microphone check (lapel mic fit and volume)
- Clicker functionality
- Video playback (if applicable)
- Confidence monitor positioning
- Q&A microphone handoff

### Booking Process
1. Rehearsal slots are pre-assigned based on your session time
2. Check your rehearsal time in the speaker portal: speaker.techconf2026.com
3. Or ask at the Speaker Registration Desk
4. Arrive 5 minutes before your slot

### If You Miss Your Slot
- Walk-in rehearsals are available between sessions
- Ask the AV technician in your room
- Minimum 5-minute quick check available

### AV Technician Contact
- In-room AV tech: Available during all session hours
- Green room: Extension 2001
- Email: av@techconf2026.com
