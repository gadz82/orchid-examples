# Speaker Check-In Process

## When to Arrive
- **Arrive at venue:** At least 2 hours before your session
- **Green room:** Open 3 hours before first session of the day
- **Rehearsal slot:** 30 minutes before your session (booked in advance)

## Check-In Steps
1. Go to the **Speaker Registration Desk** — 2nd Floor, VIP Zone
2. Present your speaker confirmation email and ID
3. Receive your **Speaker VIP Badge** (all-access)
4. Visit the **Green Room** to store belongings
5. Check in with the **AV technician** in your assigned room
6. Do a quick **mic and slide test** during your rehearsal slot

## Speaker VIP Badge Benefits
- Access to all rooms and tracks
- Speaker Green Room (2nd Floor, VIP Zone)
- Speaker Lounge with complimentary food/drinks
- Priority seating at all sessions
- Access to Speaker Dinner (Day 2, 20:00)
