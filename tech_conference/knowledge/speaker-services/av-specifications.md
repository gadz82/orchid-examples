# AV Equipment Specifications

## All Track Rooms (A, B, C, D, E)

### Standard Setup
- **Projector:** 4K laser projector, 16:9 aspect ratio
- **Screen:** 4m wide motorized screen
- **Microphone:** 1x lapel mic + 1x handheld for Q&A
- **Clicker:** Logitech Spotlight (provided, or bring your own)
- **Monitor:** 27" confidence monitor facing speaker
- **Laptop:** HDMI + USB-C connections available

### Slide Requirements
- **Format:** PDF or PPTX (16:9 ratio)
- **Submission deadline:** 48 hours before session
- **Upload portal:** speaker.techconf2026.com/slides
- **On-site backup:** USB drive accepted at green room

### Special Requests
- Live demo: Request ethernet connection 24h in advance
- Video playback: Provide files in MP4 (H.264) format
- Dual screen: Available in Main Hall only
- Live streaming: All sessions recorded, opt-out at check-in

## Workshop Labs (1, 2)

- **Setup:** 50 workstations with monitors, keyboards, mice
- **OS:** Ubuntu 24.04 LTS (pre-configured)
- **Network:** Dedicated VLAN, internet access
- **Power:** All seats have power outlets
- **Bring your own device:** HDMI input available at front

## Main Hall (Keynotes)

- **Projector:** Dual 8K laser projectors with edge blending
- **Stage:** 12m wide, 1m elevated
- **Teleprompter:** Available on request
- **Audio:** Professional PA system, lavalier + headset options
- **Recording:** Multi-camera, 4K broadcast quality
