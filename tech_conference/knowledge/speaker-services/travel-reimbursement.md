# Travel Reimbursement

## Travel Reimbursement for Speakers

### Eligibility
- All invited speakers are eligible for travel reimbursement
- Reimbursement covers: flights, train tickets, hotel (up to 3 nights)

### Reimbursement Limits
- **International flights:** Up to €800
- **Domestic flights:** Up to €300
- **Train tickets:** Up to €200
- **Hotel:** Up to €150/night (maximum 3 nights)

### How to Claim
1. Keep all receipts and boarding passes
2. Fill out the reimbursement form at the Speaker Registration Desk
3. Submit receipts and form before leaving the conference
4. Reimbursement processed within 30 business days

### Required Documents
- Original receipts (not copies)
- Boarding passes (for flights)
- Hotel invoice
- Completed reimbursement form
- Bank details for wire transfer (IBAN)

### Advance Payment
- Speakers can request advance payment for flights
- Request at least 4 weeks before the conference
- Email: travel@techconf2026.com

### Contact
- Travel coordinator: travel@techconf2026.com
- On-site: Speaker Registration Desk, 2nd Floor, VIP Zone
