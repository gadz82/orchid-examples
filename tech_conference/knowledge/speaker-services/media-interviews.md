# Media Interviews

## Media Interview Scheduling

### How to Schedule
- **Location:** Press Room, 2nd Floor, VIP Zone
- **Sign-up:** Interview scheduling desk inside the Press Room
- **Or email:** press@techconf2026.com

### Interview Formats
- **One-on-one:** 15-minute slots with individual journalists
- **Panel interview:** 30-minute group sessions (multiple journalists)
- **Video interview:** 10-minute recorded segments for conference highlights

### Availability
- Interviews can be scheduled during breaks or after sessions
- Speakers are not obligated to do interviews
- You can decline any interview request

### Preparation
- Press kits available at the Press Room
- Speaker bios and session abstracts provided to journalists
- You can request talking points in advance

### Recording Policy
- All interviews are on the record unless otherwise agreed
- You can request a review of quotes before publication
- Video interviews require signed release form
