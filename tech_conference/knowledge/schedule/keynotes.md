# Keynote Speakers

## Dr. Sarah Chen — "The Future of AI Agents"
**Day 1, 09:30-10:30, Main Hall**

AI researcher and author of "Agent Architectures," former lead at DeepMind. Dr. Chen's work focuses on multi-agent orchestration, tool use, and real-world deployment patterns for autonomous AI systems. She has published over 50 papers and holds 12 patents in AI systems.

**Session:** A deep dive into the next generation of autonomous AI agents, covering multi-agent orchestration, tool use, and real-world deployment patterns.

## Prof. Elena Vasquez — "Building Resilient Systems"
**Day 2, 09:30-10:30, Main Hall**

Professor of Distributed Systems at MIT. Prof. Vasquez is a leading expert in fault tolerance, distributed consensus, and resilient architecture. She previously worked at Google on Spanner and Borg.

**Session:** Lessons learned from building systems that survive failures, scale to millions of users, and maintain consistency under pressure.

## Alex Thompson — "Developer Experience in 2026"
**Day 3, 09:30-10:30, Main Hall**

VP of Engineering at Vercel and creator of several popular open-source developer tools. Alex is known for advocating developer-first tooling and reducing friction in the development workflow.

**Session:** How the developer experience landscape has evolved, from local development to cloud-native workflows, and what's coming next.

## Closing Keynote Panel — "What's Next"
**Day 3, 14:30-15:15, Main Hall**

All keynote speakers return for a forward-looking panel discussion on the future of technology, open source, and the developer community.
