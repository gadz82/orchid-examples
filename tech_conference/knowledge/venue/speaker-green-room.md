# Speaker Green Room

## Location
- **Floor:** 2nd Floor
- **Zone:** VIP Zone
- **Capacity:** 30 people
- **Nearest Entrance:** VIP Elevator (requires Speaker VIP Badge)
- **Nearest Stairs:** Stair V1 (VIP corridor)

## Directions from Registration Desk
1. From registration, go to the Speaker Registration Desk — 2nd Floor, VIP Zone
2. Check in and receive your Speaker VIP Badge
3. Take the VIP Elevator or Stair V1 to the Green Room area
4. The Green Room is at the end of the VIP corridor

## Amenities
- Complimentary coffee, tea, snacks, and water
- Quiet area for preparation
- Wi-Fi: `TechConf2026-VIP` / Password: `speaker-lounge-2026`
- Phone: Extension 2001
- Private restrooms
- Wardrobe area with mirror and lighting

## Hours
- Opens 3 hours before the first session of the day
- Closes 30 minutes after the last session of the day

## Accessibility
- Wheelchair accessible via VIP Elevator
- Speaker VIP Badge required for access
