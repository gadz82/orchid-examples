# Press Room

## Location
- **Floor:** 2nd Floor
- **Zone:** VIP Zone
- **Capacity:** 20 people
- **Nearest Entrance:** VIP Elevator (requires Press or Speaker Badge)
- **Nearest Stairs:** Stair V1 (VIP corridor)

## Directions from Registration Desk
1. From registration, go to the Speaker/Press Registration Desk — 2nd Floor, VIP Zone
2. Check in and receive your Press Badge
3. Take the VIP Elevator or Stair V1 to the VIP Zone
4. The Press Room is adjacent to the Speaker Green Room

## Amenities
- High-speed internet: `TechConf2026-Press` / Password: `press-2026`
- Power outlets at every seat
- Printer and scanner
- Quiet workspace for writing
- Complimentary coffee and water

## Hours
- Opens 1 hour before the first session
- Closes 1 hour after the last session

## Media Interviews
- Interview scheduling desk inside the Press Room
- Contact: press@techconf2026.com
- On-site media coordinator available during conference hours

## Accessibility
- Wheelchair accessible via VIP Elevator
- Press Badge required for access
