# Charging Stations and Device Lockers

## Charging Stations

### Locations
- **Main Hall:** Along the corridor walls on both sides
- **Room A-E:** Along the corridor wall outside each room
- **Networking Lounge:** Built into seating areas
- **Exhibition Hall:** Along the perimeter walls
- **Workshop Labs:** Every workstation has power outlets

### Types
- **USB-C:** Available at all stations
- **USB-A:** Available at most stations
- **Power outlets (EU standard):** Available at all stations
- **Wireless charging pads:** Networking Lounge seating areas

### Device Lockers

### Location
- **Lockers:** Ground Floor, near the Registration Desk

### How to Use
1. Bring your device to the locker area
2. Choose an available locker
3. Set a 4-digit PIN
4. Store your device and lock it
5. Retrieve with your PIN

### Details
- Free of charge
- 50 lockers available
- Various sizes (phone, tablet, laptop)
- Conference is not responsible for lost or damaged items
- Lockers must be emptied by end of each conference day
