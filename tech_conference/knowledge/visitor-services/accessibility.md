# Accessibility Services

## Accessibility at TechConf 2026

### Venue Accessibility
- All floors accessible via elevator
- Wide doorways and corridors throughout
- Accessible restrooms on every floor
- Reserved seating in all rooms (front rows)

### Services Available

#### Sign Language Interpretation
- Available for all keynotes and panel discussions
- Request for other sessions: contact accessibility@techconf2026.com
- 48 hours notice preferred

#### Wheelchair Access
- All rooms and facilities are wheelchair accessible
- Reserved parking near Entrance B
- Elevators in all wings

#### Quiet Room
- **Location:** Ground Floor, adjacent to Networking Lounge
- **Purpose:** Sensory break space for attendees who need a quiet environment
- **Hours:** Open during all conference hours
- **Features:** Dim lighting, comfortable seating, noise-canceling headphones available

#### Live Captioning
- Available on all screens in track rooms
- Real-time transcription for keynotes and panels

#### Assistance Animals
- Service animals welcome throughout the venue
- Water bowls available at the information desk

### Contact
- Accessibility coordinator: accessibility@techconf2026.com
- On-site: Information desk, any time during conference hours
- Phone: +39-06-1234-9900

### Pre-Conference Requests
- Email accessibility@techconf2026.com with your needs
- We accommodate all reasonable requests
- 2 weeks notice preferred for specialized arrangements
