# Registration and Badge Pickup

## Registration Process

### When to Arrive
- **Day 1:** Registration opens at 08:00
- **Day 2-3:** Registration desk open from 08:00

### Where to Go
- **Registration Desk:** Ground Floor, Main Entrance (straight ahead from the doors)

### What to Bring
- Confirmation email (printed or on phone)
- Valid ID (passport or national identity card)
- For speakers: speaker confirmation email

### Badge Types
- **Attendee Badge:** Full conference access, all rooms and tracks
- **Speaker VIP Badge:** All-access plus green room, speaker lounge, speaker dinner
- **Press Badge:** Press room access, interview scheduling
- **Workshop-Only Badge:** Workshop access only (limited track access)

### Badge Pickup Steps
1. Go to the Registration Desk
2. Present your confirmation email and ID
3. Receive your badge and conference materials
4. Badges must be worn at all times

### Lost Badge
- Report to the Registration Desk immediately
- Replacement fee: €10
- Bring ID for verification

### On-Site Registration
- Limited spots available for walk-in registration
- Price: €150/day (vs. pre-registered €99/day)
- Subject to availability
