# Transportation and Parking

## Getting to the Venue

### By Metro
- **Line A:** Stop "Congress Center" — 2-minute walk
- **Line B:** Stop "Termini" — 10-minute walk or 1 metro stop transfer

### By Bus
- Lines 40, 64, 70, 170 — Stop "Congress Center"
- Night bus N8 — Stop "Congress Center"

### By Train
- **Roma Termini Station:** 10-minute walk or 1 metro stop (Line A)
- **Roma Tiburtina:** 15-minute metro ride (Line B to A transfer)

### By Car
- **Address:** Viale della Conferenza 1, 00100 Roma
- **Parking:** See parking section below
- **ZTL (Limited Traffic Zone):** The venue is outside the ZTL — no permit needed

## Parking

### Conference Parking
- **Underground Garage:** Entrance B, €2/hour, daily max €15
- **Surface Lot:** Entrance C, €1.50/hour, 06:00-22:00
- **Disabled Parking:** Reserved spots near Entrance B (valid permit required)

### Nearby Parking
- **Parcheggio Termini:** 5-minute walk, €20/day
- **Parcheggio Esedra:** 8-minute walk, €18/day

### Electric Vehicle Charging
- 4 charging stations in the underground garage
- €0.30/kWh, app-based payment

## Shuttle Service
- **Airport to venue:** Fiumicino Express shuttle, €15 one-way
- **Hotel to venue:** See hotels-shuttles.md for hotel shuttle schedules
- **Conference app:** Real-time shuttle tracking
