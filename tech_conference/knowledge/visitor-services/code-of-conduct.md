# Code of Conduct

## TechConf 2026 Code of Conduct

### Our Pledge
TechConf 2026 is dedicated to providing a harassment-free conference experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, age, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, religion (or lack thereof), or technology choices.

### Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and inclusive
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be mindful of your surroundings and fellow participants
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback

### Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form
- Offensive verbal or written comments
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following
- Harassing photography or recording
- Sustained disruption of talks or other events
- Inappropriate physical contact
- Unwelcome sexual attention
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the above behavior

### Reporting
- **In person:** Go to any staff member (identified by red lanyards)
- **Email:** conduct@techconf2026.com
- **Anonymous:** Suggestion boxes at the information desk
- **Phone:** +39-06-1234-9910

### Enforcement
- Reports will be reviewed by the Code of Conduct committee
- Violators may be warned, asked to leave, or banned from future events
- No retaliation against those who report violations
- All reports are handled confidentially

### Scope
This code of conduct applies to all conference spaces, including talks, workshops, social events, online platforms, and one-on-one communications pertaining to conference business.
