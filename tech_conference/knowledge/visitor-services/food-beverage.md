# Food and Beverage

## Food Options at TechConf 2026

### Lunch
- **Location:** Exhibition Hall, Ground Floor, South Wing
- **Times:** 12:00 - 13:30 (all days)
- **Format:** Hot buffet with multiple stations
- **Options:**
  - Hot dishes (rotating daily)
  - Salad bar
  - Sandwich station
  - Dessert table
- **Dietary Options:** Vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free clearly labeled
- **Dietary Requests:** Noted during registration, check with staff at the food station

### Coffee Breaks
- **Morning:** 10:30 - 11:00
- **Afternoon:** 16:00 - 16:30
- **Locations:** Networking Lounge + North/South Wing lobbies
- **Includes:** Coffee, tea, pastries, fruit, water

### All Day
- **Water fountains:** On every floor
- **Vending machines:** Basement near Workshop Labs
- **Nearby restaurants:** List available at the info desk

### Evening Events
- **Welcome Party (Day 1):** Rooftop Terrace, 19:00-22:00 — finger food and drinks
- **Speaker Dinner (Day 2):** Restaurant "La Terrazza", 20:00 — speakers only

### Dietary Accommodations
- All dietary restrictions noted during registration are accommodated
- Halal, kosher, and allergy-specific meals available on request
- Speak to the catering staff at the food station for assistance
